# excel-dashboard
Sales Distribution Dashboard
This Excel-based dashboard provides a visual representation of sales distribution data across various countries. The data is displayed using multiple visualization elements, including bar charts, pie charts, and line graphs, allowing for a clear understanding of customer distribution, salary, and unit prices across different regions.
Key Features:
Country-wise Customer Count: A breakdown of customer distribution by country, shown in a bar chart and tabular format.
Total Salary vs. Quantity: A line chart compares the sum of total salaries against product quantity across different product categories.
Unit Price by Month: A pie chart visualizing the sum of unit prices for various products on a monthly basis.
Customer Insights: Focused visual representations of customer counts in selected countries (Bangladesh and the USA).
This dashboard is built to give insights into sales and distribution patterns, making it easy for users to track performance across different regions and time frames.
